Privacy Policy

Meal Planner and Lift Log · Last updated 10 October 2026

Meal Planner and Lift Log are personal apps built and used by one person, Brad White, for his own meal planning and training. They are not offered to the public. This page explains what data they access and how it is handled.

Who can use these apps

Only the owner's Google account is authorised to sign in. Anyone else who opens the web pages gets an empty app with no access to any data.

What data is accessed

  • Google Drive (Meal Planner): reading and writing files in the owner's own Meal Planner data folders, used to sync meal plans and logs between the phone and the owner's Drive.
  • Google Health / Fitbit (Lift Log): read-only access to the owner's own sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, steps and exercise data, used to review training recovery.
  • App data on the phone: meal plans, recipes, shopping lists and workout logs are stored in the phone's browser storage.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to run these apps and to review the owner's own fitness and nutrition.
  • Synced data is written to the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets. It is not sent to any server operated by the apps; there is none.
  • Access tokens are kept in memory on the phone or in the owner's own Google Apps Script project, never in the public code.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of Google user data

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, or with the Sign out option in each app. Data in Drive and Sheets can be deleted there directly.
